AI-PROOF ASSIGNMENT SUGGESTION: Minimum Robustness
============================================================

Strategy: Consolidate 8 binary categories into 6 weighted ones; add a personal-relevance criterion requiring student-specific reasoning that AI cannot generalize without detection.

RUBRIC CHANGES:
----------------------------------------

1. Policy Solutions (1 & 2) Description (2 pts)
   Description: Clearly identifies and thoroughly describes two distinct, meaningfully different proposed policy solutions addressing the chosen problem.
   Change: Merged old Solution 1 + Solution 2 categories; doubled weight.

2. Source Authority (Both Solutions) (2 pts)
   Description: Indicates and describes the expert or authoritative qualities of the source or advocate group for both Solution 1 and Solution 2.
   Change: Merged old Authority 1 + Authority 2; doubled weight.

3. Citations & Accessibility (Both Solutions) (1 pts)
   Description: Provides clear, accessible sources for both solutions that the instructor can independently locate and verify.
   Change: Merged old Sources 1 + Sources 2; kept at 1 pt (AI-completable).

4. Impact Analysis (Both Solutions) (2 pts)
   Description: Clearly identifies positive or negative potential impacts of both solutions on the chosen issue, with at least one contrast drawn between them.
   Change: Merged old Impact 1 + Impact 2; added comparative element, doubled weight.

5. Personal Relevance & Course Connection (2 pts)
   Description: Explains, in the student's own voice, why this specific issue and these specific solutions matter to them personally OR connects the solutions to a concept, reading, or discussion from this course. Generic or vague statements earn 0.
   Change: NEW category; student-specific content AI cannot reliably fabricate.

6. Solution Distinction Justification (1 pts)
   Description: Briefly explains, in the student's own words, why the two solutions are meaningfully different from each other — not merely restating descriptions.
   Change: NEW category; requires comparative original reasoning AI typically skips.


ASSIGNMENT INSTRUCTION CHANGES:
----------------------------------------

1. Add to instructions: 'Your submission must include a short paragraph (3–5 sentences) explaining why this issue personally matters to you OR connecting your solutions to a specific course reading or class discussion. Vague or generic statements will not receive credit for the Personal Relevance category.'

AI-PROOF ASSIGNMENT SUGGESTION: Heightened Security
============================================================

Strategy: Adds process evidence, personal connection, and solution comparison to expose AI copy-paste and light edits. AI-vulnerable factual categories capped to keep ceiling at 70%.

RUBRIC CHANGES:
----------------------------------------

1. Policy Solutions (1 & 2) — Description, Authority & Sources (6 pts)
   Description: Identifies both solutions with source authority and accessible citations.
   Change: Consolidates 6 original binary categories; AI can fully earn this.

2. Solutions Impact (1 & 2) (1 pts)
   Description: Clearly states positive/negative impacts of both solutions on the chosen issue.
   Change: Consolidates 2 original impact categories; AI can earn this.

3. Source Engagement Log (3 pts)
   Description: Student lists 2–3 specific details (date accessed, paywalls hit, dead links, search terms used) showing real navigation of sources.
   Change: Process artifact; AI cannot fabricate authentic browsing friction.

4. Personal Issue Connection (3 pts)
   Description: 1–2 sentences explain why this issue matters to the student personally, referencing a specific lived experience or local context.
   Change: Requires genuine autobiography; AI output is generic and detectable.

5. Solution Comparison & Preference (3 pts)
   Description: Student directly compares both solutions and states which they find more promising, with a reason tied to their specific community or circumstances.
   Change: Situated judgment; AI hedges generically and scores low here.

6. Instructor Check-In Question (4 pts)
   Description: Student answers one short instructor-posed question unique to their chosen issue (released at submission time).
   Change: Just-in-time prompt makes pre-generated AI submissions non-responsive.


ASSIGNMENT INSTRUCTION CHANGES:
----------------------------------------

1. Add a brief 'Source Engagement Log' field to the submission form (date accessed, search terms, or access obstacles for each source).

2. Release one unique 'Check-In Question' per issue topic at the time the assignment opens; students must answer it in their submission.

AI-PROOF ASSIGNMENT SUGGESTION: Best Fit
============================================================

Strategy: Consolidate redundant parallel criteria into solution summaries, then add a personal-issue connection and a head-to-head comparative judgment layer — tasks AI cannot ground in the student's own context.

RUBRIC CHANGES:
----------------------------------------

1. Solution 1 Summary & Source (2 pts)
   Description: Clearly describes Solution 1 and provides an accessible, credible source.
   Change: Merges old Solution 1 + Authority + Sources into one criterion.

2. Solution 2 Summary & Source (2 pts)
   Description: Clearly describes Solution 2 and provides an accessible, credible source.
   Change: Merges old Solution 2 + Authority + Sources into one criterion.

3. Source Authority Justification (2 pts)
   Description: Student explains in their own words *why* each source qualifies as expert or authoritative for this specific issue — not just labels them.
   Change: Elevates authority from checkbox to justified reasoning; AI struggles without personal context.

4. Personal Issue Connection (3 pts)
   Description: Student explicitly ties both solutions to why *they* chose this issue — referencing their own community, experience, or prior research.
   Change: New criterion; requires personal grounding AI cannot authentically replicate.

5. Comparative Impact Judgment (3 pts)
   Description: Student directly compares the two solutions' potential impacts and defends which addresses the problem more effectively and why.
   Change: New criterion; demands original evaluative reasoning, not parallel description.

6. Research Process Reflection (3 pts)
   Description: Student briefly describes how they found and evaluated sources — including at least one source they considered but rejected and why.
   Change: New criterion; process evidence is impossible for AI to fabricate authentically.


ASSIGNMENT INSTRUCTION CHANGES:
----------------------------------------

1. Add a short mandatory header (3–5 sentences) before the submission body: student states their chosen issue, why it matters to them personally, and what they already knew about it before researching — this anchors the personal connection and process reflection criteria and must be written in first person.
